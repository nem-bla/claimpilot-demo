The problem

The care is documented. The packet is not.

Families hire you because the health system will not assemble itself. Then a Genworth, John Hancock, or Northwestern Mutual policy appears, and the work changes shape.

  • Read a 40–80 page contract for this policy’s elimination period, not a generic 90-day rule.
  • Prove two ADLs or a cognitive impairment the way this carrier defines them.
  • Show that the aide, the agency, or the facility is an eligible provider.
  • Get a physician statement that matches the claim form, not a progress note.
  • Submit invoices and care notes in a format intake will not kick back.
  • Track notice-of-claim, proof-of-loss, assessment, and appeal dates so the file does not go quiet.
How it works

Four steps. Every extracted fact points back to a page.

1

Ingest

Upload the policy, the claim kit if the carrier sent one, and the care documents you already produce: assessments, plans of care, visit notes, invoices, physician letters.

2

Cited extraction

Benefit triggers, elimination period, daily maximum, eligible settings, licensed-provider rules, deadlines — each tied to the source page. Nothing is a free-floating summary.

3

Packet

A carrier-shaped checklist, completed forms where the source supports them, a citation appendix, and care evidence mapped to ADLs. Gaps are listed as gaps, not filled in.

4

Deadlines

Notice of claim, proof of loss, assessments, invoice cadence, and appeal windows on one timeline. The software does not call the carrier. It keeps the file from missing the date.

During the design-partner period we work from conversations and redacted or synthetic policies. Real protected health information is not ingested until a Business Associate Agreement is in place.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this insurance advice?

No. ClaimPilot extracts and organizes what the policy and the care record already say. Coverage decisions belong to the carrier. We do not interpret “should this be paid?” We assemble the file.

Is this a chatbot?

No. There is no conversational agent talking to families or to carriers. The product is ingest → cited extraction → packet → deadlines.

Will you handle real medical records?

Not until a Business Associate Agreement is signed. Design-partner work starts with conversation and with redacted or synthetic policies.

Do you call Genworth / John Hancock / Northwestern Mutual for us?

No. We produce the packet those calls are about.
